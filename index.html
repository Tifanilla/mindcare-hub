Core Insights

📖 Definition

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act.

🔍 Causes

Rooted in a mix of biological factors (genes/brain chemistry), life experiences (trauma/abuse), and family history.

⚡ Effects

Can impact physical health, decision-making, relationships, and everyday productivity if left unaddressed.

🛡️ Prevention

Practicing mindfulness, staying connected, getting adequate rest, and setting healthy boundaries.
