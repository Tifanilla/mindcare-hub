Understanding Mental Health

Foundations of well-being, root causes, impacts, and pathways forward.

📖 Meaning

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It influences how we think, feel, act, handle stress, relate to others, and make healthy decisions.

⚠️ Causes

Challenges can arise from biological factors (gene history, brain chemistry), life experiences (trauma, ongoing stress, major life changes), or environmental pressures.

🌊 Effects

Unmanaged distress can impact physical health, disrupt sleep patterns, strain interpersonal relationships, reduce work/study performance, and cause social isolation.

💡 Solutions

Recovery is supported by professional therapy, self-care routines, building dependable support systems, practicing mindfulness, and community connection.
