What is Mental Health?

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make healthy choices. Mental health is important at every stage of life, from childhood and adolescence through adulthood.

Causes & Effects

🔍 Common Causes

  • Biological factors: Genes or brain chemistry.
  • Life experiences: Trauma, abuse, or chronic stress.
  • Family history: Having a history of mental health conditions.
  • Environmental factors: Isolation, financial strain, or heavy pressure.

⚡ Potential Effects

  • Impact on physical health (fatigue, headaches, sleep issues).
  • Difficulty in maintaining personal or professional relationships.
  • Reduced productivity and focus at work or school.
  • Overwhelming feelings of anxiety, sadness, or burnout.

Preventions & Healthy Habits

🧘 Mindfulness & Stress Management

Practicing deep breathing, meditation, or yoga to keep your nervous system grounded.

💤 Quality Sleep Hygiene

Aiming for 7-9 hours of consistent sleep nightly to allow the brain to recover and repair.

🤝 Strong Social Support

Maintaining open communication with friends, family, or trusted community groups.
